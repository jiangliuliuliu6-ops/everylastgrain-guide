FIELD NOTES / GUIDE

Your first dig in Every Last Grain

The free Lot Edge demo is a loop of digging, selling, and coming back with better equipment. Here is what to do before you chase every buried object.

Controls

Use WASD to move and the mouse to look. Hold left click or E while aiming at sand to dig. Hold Shift to sprint while you have stamina. Press J for the Journal and P for the menu.

The official demo is designed for mouse and keyboard. If the pointer stops controlling the camera, click inside the game again.

The first three trips

  1. Fill the starter bag. Scoop nearby sand until your carrying limit is reached.
  2. Sell at the Sand Deposit. Walk to it and use E to turn the load into cash.
  3. Upgrade at the Supply Terminal. Compare tools and Bags & Tanks before spending. A faster tool fills a tiny bag quickly, so capacity matters too.

Repeat the loop. As more sand clears, you can explore lower layers and look for finds.

Looking for the 14 finds

The official game page confirms 14 buried finds, including one special discovery. The creator says valuable finds are buried deeper. Explore beyond the surface and use the Journal to review your collection.

Our local finds tracker lets you mark discovered slots and write your own clues. It does not read or change the game's save.

Where to play

Oliver Kuso's Update 12 asks third-party sites to link to his official itch.io page. For the creator-hosted version, open the official demo in a desktop browser and use fullscreen there.

Sources: official game page, Update 3, and Update 12. Checked November 27, 2026.
